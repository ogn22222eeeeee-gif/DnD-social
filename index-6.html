Set up creator payouts

Choose a supported payment provider and complete its official onboarding, identity verification, and payout setup. Availability depends on your country and provider approval.

Open Paystack official website ↗Open Flutterwave official website ↗

These links open provider websites; they do not connect a payout account to DND by themselves.

How DND monetization will work

  1. Creator signs up and completes required verification.
  2. DND records eligible revenue using configured rules and provider events.
  3. Creator sees verified earnings in their dashboard.
  4. Creator requests payout to a verified payout method.
  5. Server checks the balance and provider confirms the payout.
Before enabling real withdrawals, we must implement server-side payment verification, secure payout requests, fraud controls, transaction records, and provider-specific requirements.

Ways creators could earn

• Eligible video or creator revenue sharing

• Viewer tips and virtual gifts

• Paid subscriptions or memberships

• Marketplace commissions, where applicable

These are planned features, not currently active revenue sources.

Banking & payments

Open your bank securely

DND does not create bank accounts or hold money. Select a provider to open its official website. Check the address before entering details.

Creator payouts — future integration

Use a licensed payment provider after business verification and secure server-side integration. Never ask users for bank passwords, card PINs, or one-time passwords.
